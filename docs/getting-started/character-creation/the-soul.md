Everything and everyone has a soul in the World of Rathion; and each soul is truly unique especially for things that are living. The most important thing before you can create your first character is how to
come up with their soul.

## WHAT IS THE SOUL
Your character's soul is what houses all of their Echoes and is something that can be interracted with by themselves and possibly others. The soul is a reflection of the character's internal thoughts, their beliefs, and
their drives. As a character grows and develops - small changes to the appearance of their Soul may occur, but this is rather atypical.

You start with the characters Soul as that is the fundamental answer to the question: "Who are you?"
